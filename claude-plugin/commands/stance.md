First call `recall` with query: "$ARGUMENTS", mode: "project". These are the
stances this project's gate enforces, including ones I added with
`/deeppairing:reject` or `deeppairing stance add`. List each one with its
reason and how it was added.

Then call `recall` with query: "$ARGUMENTS", mode: "philosophy" for my
cross-project ledger.

If there's an 'avoid' stance, walk me through:
- What I rejected and why (latest reason)
- Whether it's enforced here (a project stance) or only advisory (cross-project)
- Whether anything I'm about to propose conflicts with it

If there's a 'prefer' stance, note it and remember it when proposing.

If both are empty for this concept, say so — I may want to think about it
fresh, rather than letting you fill in from first principles.
